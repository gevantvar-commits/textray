Textray Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Short version: Textray works entirely on your iPhone. Your screenshots and chats are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

What we process

  • Screenshots and text you choose to analyze are processed on your device using Apple's Vision framework and, where available, Apple's on-device Foundation Models.
  • We do not operate servers that receive your chats, screenshots or results.

What is stored

  • Your scan history (results and the screenshots you analyzed) is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. Delete it any time in Settings › Delete All Scan History, or by deleting the app.
  • Your onboarding answers are stored locally to tailor suggestions.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Advertising measurement

Textray uses Apple's privacy-preserving AdAttributionKit to report anonymous, aggregated install and purchase counts to ad networks. No personal data, chat content or device identifiers are shared, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Textray is not directed at children under 13.
